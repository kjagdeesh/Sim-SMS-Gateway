Turn your Android phone into a private SMS gateway.

Sim SMS Gateway runs a secure-by-design REST API on your phone. Send single or bulk SMS from any website or backend through your own SIM and plan — and see every message as success or failed.

Why Sim SMS Gateway

Everything you need to send SMS from your own number

Single & bulk sending

Message one person or up to 100 recipients in a single request, with a clear result for every number.

Live status dashboard

Track total, success and failed messages with filters and a full history stored on the device.

Developer-friendly API

JSON over HTTP with an OpenAPI spec, copy-ready cURL, JavaScript and Python examples and a live tester.

Private by design

Messages go from your app to your phone and out through your SIM. No third party reads your content.

Country-aware numbers

Pick a country once and local numbers are converted to international format automatically.

Persistent history

Every attempt is saved in a SQLite database, so the dashboard shows your previous messages anytime.

How it works

From your app to the carrier network in four steps

Your app or websiteCalls the REST API
Gateway APIValidates and authenticates
Android phoneHands off to SMS manager
Your SIM & carrierDelivers the SMS

    Good to know

    • The gateway speaks plain HTTP. Use it on a trusted network and never expose the port to the public internet.
    • A website hosted over HTTPS cannot call an HTTP phone address; browsers block mixed content. Use this dashboard locally or put the phone behind a secure tunnel.
    • Carrier charges apply to every SMS. Only message people who agreed to receive it.
